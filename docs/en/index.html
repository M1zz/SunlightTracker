What is it?

Sunny Sunflower is an iOS app that uses your camera’s light sensing to detect real sunlight automatically and log it for you. The more sun you get, the more your battery charges, and that energy helps your very own sunflower grow. Forget to look after it, though, and it will start to wilt. 🌱

  • ☀️ Automatic sunlight detection and logging based on light levels
  • 🌻 Charge your battery, then grow your sunflower
  • 📅 Review your history in the calendar and stats, and add moods and notes
  • 👥 Soak up the sun near a friend and your petals turn colorful
  • 📱 Home Screen widgets, Lock Screen Live Activities, and the Dynamic Island

What about my privacy?

No servers · your logs stay on your device

Nothing you log in Sunny Sunflower ever leaves your device. Only feedback you choose to send reaches the developer. For details, see our Privacy Policy.
